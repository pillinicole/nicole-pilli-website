RBC Royal Bank of Canada

Payments Technologies

Overview

I started my career as a delivery leader in digital payments at Canada’s leading bank, RBC, where I honed my skills in managing large teams, big project budgets, and complex architecture. I moved from small to large digital transformation projects that spanned multiple channels in banking technology. Here, I delivered projects that enabled small and large business banking clients to create, track, and organize payments through various payments rails.

RBC Edge (2020-2022)

Delivering Payments Capabilities for the New Business Banking Portal: Account Transfers, Bill Payments, E-Transfers, Wire Payments

Led a large team (20+) on a multi-year program initiative: a brand new business banking portal using new front and back-end systems.

RBC PayEdge (2019-2020)

Managed the acquisition and code migration of an external vendor for Accounts Payable and B2B payments, “WayPay,” into the enterprise system. Managed cloud security & risk assessments, including working with Enterprise Cloud security and third-party cloud architects, and led migration of one of the first projects on AWS public cloud to enter the bank.

RBC Express Swift Wire Tracking (2018-2019)

End-to-End launch of Swift Wire Tracking (opens in a new tab) (~$100k)

Enabled business clients to have an end-to-end view of their wire payment, resulting in fewer discrepancies and customer complaints or inquiries
