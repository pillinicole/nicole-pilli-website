About

I'm a delivery leader with 8+ years experience across software delivery, digital product development, game production, and release management. I have a proven track record in leading end-to-end project delivery in large, cross-functional teams; ensuring timely launches and community satisfaction through numerous live updates and significant project budgets. My background scope spans across payments banking systems, PC and mobile gaming, web development, and blockchain technology.

People First

For me, every product relates back to people: the people who use it, and/or the people who build it. I take a “people first” approach to project delivery in all cases; seeing through the eye of the customer, and enabling the harmony of the team.

Trust, communication, and collaboration are the three values that I instill to get things done.

Happy teams = better products.

Problem Solving

I am there to help my team solve problems. If we need to pivot, we find the solution to that problem in real-time. I am a steward for my teams and ensure they have everything they need to understand the task at hand, get the answers they need, and get their job done, effectively.

Team Conduit

I am the conduit between the technical team and external stakeholders. I have an expert ability to translate what the team is working on into tangible action steps and elements that non-technical folks can understand. I have presented to directors, VPs, Senior VPs, and C-suite executives and worked with varying subject matter experts that range from devOps, cyber security, cloud security, blockchain, network engineering, UX design, animators, tech artists, and more!
